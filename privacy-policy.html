Privacy & Data Governance

BharatDarshan Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026. This Privacy Policy outlines how BharatDarshan.online treats user data, cookies, and analytics.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BharatDarshan.online is a static travel resource. We do not require accounts, logins, passwords, credit card numbers, or personal identifying information to browse our travel destination guides, itineraries, or tools.

2. Log Files & Standard Web Metrics

Like most web platforms hosted on modern infrastructure, server logs may record basic diagnostic data including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These logs are non-personally identifiable and are utilized exclusively to assess technical health and maintain fast delivery.

3. Cookies and Advertising (Google AdSense)

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to BharatDarshan and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Client-Side Data Handling

Interactive utilities like our trip planning calculator operate entirely within your browser's local memory. No entered trip preferences, party sizes, or destinations are transmitted to or stored on remote databases.

5. External Web Links

Our articles contain hyperlinks to external sites, such as the Indian Railways portal (irctc.co.in) or state tourism websites. BharatDarshan is not responsible for the privacy practices of external domains.
