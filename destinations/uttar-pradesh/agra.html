Agra Travel Guide: The Taj Mahal, Historic Forts & Complete Itinerary

The iconic ivory-white marble Taj Mahal reflected in the central reflecting pool in Agra, Uttar Pradesh.
State & Region Uttar Pradesh (Braj / West)
Best Time to Visit October to March
Ideal Trip Duration 1 to 2 Days
Nearest Airport Agra Airport (AGR) / Delhi (DEL)
Nearest Rail Terminal Agra Cantt (AGC) / Agra Fort (AF)
Weekly Closure Taj Mahal closed on Fridays

Why Visit Agra?

Agra, resting gracefully along the banks of the Yamuna River in western Uttar Pradesh, was the epicenter of the Mughal Empire under emperors Akbar, Jahangir, and Shah Jahan. It is home to three UNESCO World Heritage Sites: the peerless Taj Mahal, the imposing red sandstone fortress of Agra Fort, and the abandoned royal city of Fatehpur Sikri.

Together with Delhi and Jaipur, Agra forms the legendary Golden Triangle tourist circuit. Beyond world-famous monuments, visitors can discover the ancient craft of Pietra Dura (semi-precious stone marble inlay), savor Agra's famous translucent Petha sweet, and photograph the Taj Mahal across the Yamuna from the serene gardens of Mehtab Bagh.

Top Places to Visit in Agra

1. The Taj Mahal Open: Sunrise to Sunset (Closed Fridays)

Commissioned in 1632 by Emperor Shah Jahan to house the tomb of his beloved empress Mumtaz Mahal, this monumental mausoleum is celebrated worldwide as the supreme masterpiece of Muslim art in India. Built entirely of pure white Makrana marble, the monument changes hue subtly from delicate pink at dawn to shimmering gold under the midday sun and luminescent silver at dusk.

Ticketing Tip: Purchase your official Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) ticket online in advance to bypass long queue lines at the West or East gates.

2. Agra Fort (Red Fort of Agra) Open: Sunrise to Sunset Daily

A colossal 16th-century fortress of red sandstone spanning 94 acres. Akbar laid its massive semicircular bastions, while Shah Jahan embellished its interior with delicate white marble pavilions, including the Diwan-i-Am, Diwan-i-Khas, and the Musamman Burj tower where Shah Jahan spent his final years gazing out at the Taj Mahal.

3. Fatehpur Sikri 37 km from Agra

Akbar's visionary red sandstone capital, founded in 1571 and abandoned due to water shortages after barely 14 years. It remains pristine, housing the towering 54-meter Buland Darwaza (Gate of Victory), the white marble shrine of Sufi saint Sheikh Salim Chishti, and Panch Mahal.

4. Mehtab Bagh (Moonlight Garden) Best: 4:30 PM – Sunset

A historic 25-acre charbagh complex situated directly across the Yamuna River from the Taj Mahal. It offers an unobstructed, peaceful panorama of the monument's rear marble dome and minarets without crowds.

5. Tomb of I'timad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj) Open Daily

Often hailed as the direct architectural precursor to the Taj Mahal, this jewel-like tomb was built by Empress Nur Jahan for her father between 1622 and 1628. It represents the transition from red sandstone to white marble inlay work.

Suggested 2-Day Agra Itinerary

Day 1: Sunrise Taj Mahal, Agra Fort & Sunset at Mehtab Bagh

Arrive early via the high-speed Gatimaan Express from Delhi (1 hr 40 min). Head straight through the East Gate of the Taj Mahal at sunrise when soft light illuminates the marble dome. After breakfast, explore the vast courtyards of Agra Fort. In the evening, cross the Yamuna bridge to catch sunset reflections at Mehtab Bagh.

Day 2: Fatehpur Sikri Excursion & Baby Taj

Take a 45-minute drive southwest to explore Fatehpur Sikri and walk through the Buland Darwaza. Return to Agra for lunch, visit the delicate marble tomb of I'timad-ud-Daulah, and explore the marble artisan workshops of Sadar Bazar before onward travel to Jaipur or Delhi.

How to Reach Agra

By Train

The fastest route is via Gatimaan Express or Vande Bharat Express from Hazrat Nizamuddin (Delhi) to Agra Cantt in just 100 minutes. Frequent Shatabdi and Intercity services run throughout the day.

By Road

The 165 km six-lane Yamuna Expressway links Greater Noida (Delhi NCR) to Agra in approximately 2.5 to 3 hours. Regular UPSRTC Volvo buses operate from Anand Vihar and Kashmere Gate terminals.

By Air

Agra Airport (Kheria Air Base) operates select domestic connections. Most international travelers fly into Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) and take an express train or taxi.
