Short Getaways from Delhi NCR

Best Weekend Trips from Delhi: Top Escapes & Transit Guide

Delhi's strategic northern hub position and its connectivity to expressways and semi-high-speed rail routes make it simple to plan refreshing 2-to-3 day getaways.

1. Agra (The Taj Mahal Getaway)

Transit Time: 1 hr 40 min via Gatimaan Express from Nizamuddin; 2.5 hrs via Yamuna Expressway.
Key Highlights: Sunrise at the Taj Mahal without crowds, touring Akbar's red sandstone Agra Fort, and enjoying sunset views across the Yamuna from Mehtab Bagh.

2. Rishikesh (Adventure & Ganga Retreat)

Transit Time: 4.5 hrs by car via Delhi-Dehradun Expressway or 4 hrs on the Vande Bharat to Haridwar.
Key Highlights: White-water river rafting in Shivpuri, attending the tranquil evening Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat, walking across Ram Jhula, and organic riverside cafe hopping in Tapovan.

3. Jaipur (Royal Heritage & Shopping)

Transit Time: 3.5 hrs via Delhi-Mumbai Expressway; 3.5 hrs via Ajmer Shatabdi / Vande Bharat.
Key Highlights: Ascending Amer Fort, photographing Hawa Mahal, watching the sunset over the city from Nahargarh, and feasting on authentic Dal Baati Churma.
