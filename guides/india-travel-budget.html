India Travel Budget Guide: Realistic Costs & Expense Planning

India remains one of the world's most accessible travel destinations for value, but costs vary widely depending on whether you choose local pilgrim dhabas or heritage palace suites. Here is a transparent breakdown of expected daily expenses.

1. Daily Budget Breakdown by Traveler Style

Travel Tier Accommodation Meals & Drinks Local Transit Approximate Daily Total
Budget Traveler ₹800 – ₹1,500 (Guesthouse / Dharamshala) ₹400 – ₹600 (Thalis, dhabas, street food) ₹200 – ₹400 (E-rickshaws, metro, public bus) ₹1,400 – ₹2,500 / day
Mid-Range Traveler ₹3,500 – ₹6,500 (3/4-Star Hotel or Haveli) ₹1,200 – ₹2,000 (Air-conditioned cafes & restaurants) ₹1,000 – ₹1,800 (App-based cabs, private day taxis) ₹5,700 – ₹10,300 / day
Luxury Traveler ₹15,000 – ₹50,000+ (5-Star Palace / Boutique Resort) ₹3,500 – ₹8,000 (Fine dining hotels) ₹3,500 – ₹6,000 (Chauffeured sedan / SUV) ₹22,000 – ₹64,000+ / day
Important Advisory: Estimated budget — actual costs vary by season, transport, accommodation, and personal preferences. Expect 25% to 50% price surges during peak winter holidays (December 20 – January 5) and major religious festivals like Diwali, Holi, and Kumbh Mela.

2. Intercity Transit Expenses

  • Train Travel (Vande Bharat / Shatabdi Chair Car): ₹900 – ₹1,800 per passenger for a 300 to 500 km journey (includes meals).
  • Overnight Train (AC 2-Tier / 3-Tier): ₹1,200 – ₹2,200 for a 700 to 1,200 km route (e.g. Delhi to Varanasi).
  • Domestic Flights: ₹3,500 – ₹7,000 when booked 3 to 6 weeks in advance on trunk routes (e.g. Delhi to Mumbai or Jaipur to Bangalore).

3. Monument Entry & Activities

Entry tickets for Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) category monuments differ for Indian citizens and foreign visitors. For Indian visitors, entry generally ranges between ₹40 and ₹50 per monument; for foreign visitors, tickets typically range between ₹300 and ₹650 (with the Taj Mahal at ₹1,100 + ₹200 for the main mausoleum).
