India Travel Packing Checklist: All Seasons & Cultural Essentials

Packing for India is all about breathable modesty, easy-to-remove footwear, and a well-curated travel health kit. Here is the verified packing inventory.

1. Clothing & Temple Attire

  • Breathable Cotton / Linen Fabrics: Loose-fitting shirts, tunics, kurtas, and lightweight trousers suited for warm days.
  • Modest Temple Coverings: Lightweight scarves or shawls (dupatta) to cover shoulders and heads when visiting temples, gurdwaras, and mosques.
  • Easy Slip-On Footwear: Comfortable sandals or slip-on shoes since footwear must be removed repeatedly at shrines and homes.
  • Winter Layers (Nov – Feb): A warm fleece or light down jacket for North India and hill station evenings, where temperatures drop to 5°C–12°C.

2. Health, Hygiene & Electronics

  • Medical Kit: Oral rehydration salts (ORS), motion sickness tablets, antacids, insect repellent, and personal prescription medications.
  • Universal Power Adapter: India uses Type D and Type C three-round-pin plugs operating on 230V / 50Hz.
  • Power Bank (10,000–20,000 mAh): Indispensable for long train journeys and day excursions when tracking navigation.
