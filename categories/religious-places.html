Spiritual Shrines & Faith

Religious Places & Sacred Pilgrimages in India

India is the cradle of major world faiths—Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism—and home to centuries-old Sufi shrines, historic mosques, and ancient basilicas. Plan visits to India's most sanctified pilgrimage centers with practical darshan advice and etiquette rules.

Uttar Pradesh
Ram Janmabhoomi

Ayodhya Holy City

Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Saryu river aarti, and the sacred pilgrimage route of the Kosala kingdom.

Ideal: 2-3 Days View Guide →
Uttar Pradesh
Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga

Varanasi (Kashi)

One of the world's oldest sacred cities, revered for Shri Kashi Vishwanath temple, Ganga ghats, and Dashashwamedh evening aarti.

Ideal: 3-4 Days View Guide →
Punjab
Sri Harmandir Sahib

Amritsar Golden Temple

The supreme spiritual sanctuary of Sikhism, featuring the 24-hour Guru ka Langar, sacred Amrit Sarovar pool, and Gurbani kirtan.

Ideal: 2-3 Days View Guide →
Uttarakhand
Yoga & Ganga Sanctity

Rishikesh Holy Town

Triveni Ghat evening Maha Aarti, Parmarth Niketan, Vedic ashrams, and the sacred threshold to the Himalayan Char Dham circuit.

Ideal: 3-4 Days View Guide →
Pilgrim Guidelines: Most major shrines require visitors to cover their heads (e.g. Golden Temple) or deposit mobile phones, smartwatches, and leather goods in designated cloakrooms (e.g. Ram Mandir, Kashi Vishwanath). Dress modestly with covered shoulders and knees.
