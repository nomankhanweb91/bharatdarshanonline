Uttar Pradesh Travel & Tourism Guide

Uttar Pradesh is the spiritual, cultural, and historical epicenter of northern India—nurtured by the sacred Ganga and Yamuna river valleys. It is home to world wonders, epic pilgrimage sanctuaries, and vibrant culinary traditions.

Travel Connectivity in Uttar Pradesh

Aviation Hubs

Operated via international airports in Lucknow (LKO), Varanasi (VNS), and the new Maharishi Valmiki Airport in Ayodhya (AYJ), offering seamless flights from across India.

Expressway Corridors

Uttar Pradesh boasts India's most extensive expressway grid: Yamuna Expressway, Agra-Lucknow Expressway, and Purvanchal Expressway linking Delhi to Ghazipur.

Vande Bharat Rail

High-speed Vande Bharat trains connect Delhi to Varanasi via Kanpur and Prayagraj, and Delhi to Ayodhya via Lucknow.
