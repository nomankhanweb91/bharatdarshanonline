How to Plan a Pilgrimage Trip in India: Protocols & Practical Tips

Visiting sacred pilgrimage centers in India—such as Ayodhya, Kashi Vishwanath in Varanasi, or the Golden Temple in Amritsar—is a profound experience that requires specific logistical preparation.

1. Understanding Darshan & Queue Protocols

Major pilgrimage shrines operate both free general queues (Samanya Darshan) and pre-booked specialized slots (Sugam Darshan / Special Entry). Always check the official shrine board website in advance:

  • Ayodhya (Ram Janmabhoomi): Free general entry operates continuously from 6:30 AM to 9:30 PM. Specific Aarti passes can be pre-reserved online through the official trust portal.
  • Varanasi (Kashi Vishwanath): Mangala Aarti passes and Sugam Darshan tickets can be pre-booked online through the Shri Kashi Vishwanath Special Area Development Board.
  • Amritsar (Golden Temple): Open 24 hours daily with no ticket requirements.

2. Strict Security & Locker Protocols

Most prominent pilgrimage complexes prohibit electronic devices inside the inner sanctum:

  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, cameras, power banks, and leather belts/wallets are strictly prohibited at Ayodhya and Kashi Vishwanath.
  • Use the official complimentary electronic locker counters available outside the security barriers before joining the queue.

3. Dress Codes & Cultural Norms

Traditional modest clothing is expected across all Indian pilgrimage sites: dhoti-kurta or pyjama-kurta for men, and saree or salwar-kameez with dupatta for women. Avoid shorts, sleeveless tops, or western beachwear in temple precincts.
