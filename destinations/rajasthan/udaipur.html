Udaipur Travel Guide: Lake Pichola, Grand Palaces & Itinerary

Why Visit Udaipur?

Established in 1559 by Maharana Udai Singh II after the Mughal siege of Chittorgarh, Udaipur served as the fortified historic capital of the Mewar kingdom. Cradled between the emerald Aravalli mountain ranges and an intricate system of interconnected freshwater lakes, Udaipur is widely acclaimed as India's most romantic city.

The destination is celebrated for majestic white-washed marble palaces, island pavilions that appear to float effortlessly on glistening waters, centuries-old stepwells, classical Mewari miniature painting ateliers, and tranquil rooftop restaurants overlooking sunset-drenched ghats.

Top Places to Visit in Udaipur

1. Udaipur City Palace Complex Open: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM

Rajasthan's largest royal palace complex, constructed over 400 years by 22 successive Maharanas. Overlooking Lake Pichola, the palace contains a labyrinth of domes, balconies, towers, and courtyards including the Mor Chowk (famed for its radiant glass mosaic peacocks), Zenana Mahal, and the Crystal Gallery housing rare European crystal treasures.

2. Lake Pichola & Sunset Boat Cruise Cruises: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

An artificial freshwater lake created in 1362 AD. A boat cruise departing from the City Palace Bansi Ghat offers magical vistas of the Lake Palace (Jag Niwas), Mohan Mandir, and the Gangaur Ghat arches.

3. Jag Mandir Island (Lake Garden Palace) Accessible by Ferry Boat

Built as a summer resort and refuge for Mughal Prince Khurram (later Emperor Shah Jahan) in the 1620s. Features stone elephant statues guarding the jetty, yellow sandstone cupolas, and flower-filled courtyards.

4. Saheliyon-ki-Bari (Courtyard of Maidens) Open Daily

An elegant garden built by Maharana Sangram Singh II for 48 royal maidens. Famous for its marble elephant fountains, lush lotus pools, and whispering rain pavilions powered entirely by gravity flow from Fateh Sagar Lake without electric pumps.

5. Monsoon Palace (Sajjangarh Fort) Best: 4:30 PM – Sunset

Perched atop Bansdara peak 944 meters above sea level, this hilltop fortress was conceived as an astronomical retreat to observe monsoon clouds. It offers a 360-degree panoramic vista across the Udaipur basin and surrounding wildlife sanctuary.

Suggested 3-Day Udaipur Itinerary

Day 1: City Palace, Jagdish Temple & Sunset Cruise

Tour the grand courtyards and galleries of the City Palace in the morning. Visit the intricately carved 1651 AD Jagdish Temple nearby. In the late afternoon, take a boat cruise across Lake Pichola, disembarking at Jag Mandir island for tea during twilight.

Day 2: Saheliyon-ki-Bari, Fateh Sagar & Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace

Begin the morning amidst the cooling fountains of Saheliyon-ki-Bari. Drive along the picturesque promenade of Fateh Sagar Lake and take a boat to Nehru Park. By late afternoon, drive up the winding hill road to Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace) for spectacular sunset views over the Aravalli peaks.

Day 3: Bagore Ki Haveli, Artisan Markets & Dharohar Dance

Explore the historic 18th-century mansion of Bagore Ki Haveli at Gangaur Ghat. Browse silver jewelry, miniature paintings, and leather journals in the old bazaars. In the evening, watch the vibrant Dharohar folk dance & puppet performance at Bagore Ki Haveli.

How to Reach Udaipur

By Air

Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) at Dabok is 22 km east of the city, with daily flights connecting Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Jaipur.

By Train

Udaipur City Railway Station (UDZ) connects directly with Delhi via Chetak Express and Mewar Express, as well as Vande Bharat Express to Jaipur and express trains to Mumbai, Ahmedabad, and Kolkata.

By Road

Located on NH 48, Udaipur connects efficiently to Ahmedabad (260 km, 4.5 hrs) and Jaipur (390 km, 6.5 hrs). Luxury sleeper buses operate round-the-clock.
