Ayodhya Travel Guide: Places to Visit, Ram Mandir & Complete Trip Planning

Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir crafted from pink Bansi Paharpur sandstone in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh.
State & Region Uttar Pradesh (Awadh)
Best Time to Visit October to March
Ideal Trip Duration 2 to 3 Days
Nearest Airport Ayodhya Airport (AYJ)
Nearest Rail Terminal Ayodhya Dham (AY) / Cantt (AYC)
Primary Languages Hindi, Awadhi

Why Visit Ayodhya?

Ayodhya, perched along the revered banks of the sacred Saryu River in eastern Uttar Pradesh, is celebrated as the legendary birthplace of Lord Rama and the historic capital of the Kosala kingdom. As one of the Mokshadaayini Saptapuri (seven sacred liberation cities in Hindu tradition), Ayodhya blends thousands of years of epic literature with monumental contemporary temple architecture.

Beyond its spiritual preeminence, Ayodhya offers an immersive atmosphere characterized by ornate 19th-century havelis, continuous Vedic chanting at ancient ghats, vibrant temple markets offering peda sweets and brass lamps, and serene sunset boat cruises on the gentle waters of the Saryu.

Top Places to Visit in Ayodhya

1. Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir Open: 6:30 AM – 9:30 PM

The grand temple complex, designed in traditional North Indian Nagara architectural style using hand-carved pink sandstone from Rajasthan's Bansi Paharpur quarries. The complex features multiple pillared mandapas (Gudh, Rang, Nritya, Kirtan, and Prarthana Mandap) and houses the sacred sanctum sanctorum (Garbhagriha).

Visitor Note: Entry is free. Electronic items and leather belongings are strictly deposited at the security cloakrooms prior to entry.

2. Hanuman Garhi Temple Open: 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM

A fortified 10th-century temple situated atop a 76-step mound in the heart of Ayodhya. Local tradition holds that one must first pay respects to Lord Hanuman—the guardian deity of Ayodhya—before proceeding to Ram Janmabhoomi. The circular fortress courtyard features an exquisite idol of young Hanuman seated on the lap of mother Anjana.

3. Kanak Bhawan Open: 8:00 AM – 11:30 AM, 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM

Often referred to as Sone-ka-Ghar (House of Gold), Kanak Bhawan was gifted to Mata Sita upon her marriage. Renovated in 1891 by Queen Vrishbhanu Kuvari of Orchha, this palace-temple exhibits Bundela architectural influences, arched verandahs, marble courtyards, and beautifully adorned deities wearing golden crowns.

4. Ram Ki Paidi & Saryu River Ghats Best: Sunrise & Sunset

A vast series of stone ghats flanking the Saryu canal system. At twilight, the grand Maha Aarti ceremony takes place with synchronized brass lamps, incense smoke, and conch shells, followed by the illuminated fountains and laser light display recounting epic narratives.

5. Nageshwarnath Temple Open: 5:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this historic shrine at Ram Ki Paidi is believed to have been established by Lord Rama's son, Kusha. It becomes the vibrant focal point for thousands of pilgrims during Maha Shivratri and the sacred month of Shravana.

Suggested 3-Day Ayodhya Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival, Hanuman Garhi & Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan

Arrive in Ayodhya via train or flight. Check into your accommodation. Begin your journey with the traditional ascent to Hanuman Garhi. Proceed through the Ram Path corridor to Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir for afternoon darshan. In the evening, witness the breathtaking Saryu Maha Aarti at Nayaghat.

Day 2: Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal & Heritage Walk

Experience early morning prayers at Kanak Bhawan. Stroll through the ancient courtyards of Dashrath Mahal and Sita Ki Rasoi. In the afternoon, enjoy a peaceful wooden boat ride on the Saryu River and explore the lush gardens of Guptar Ghat, associated with Lord Rama's divine Jal Samadhi.

Day 3: Surya Kund, Local Markets & Departure

Visit the restored ancient water reservoir of Surya Kund, known for its sound and light installations. Browse local bazaars along Dharam Path for traditional brassware, wooden souvenirs, and local sweets before proceeding to the airport or railway terminal.

How to Reach Ayodhya

By Air

Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ) is located approximately 10 km from the city center, offering regular non-stop flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, and Kolkata. Pre-paid taxis and electric autos are readily available outside the terminal.

By Train

Ayodhya is served by two major railway junctions: Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY) (walking distance to Ram Path) and Ayodhya Cantt (AYC) (8 km away). Both stations are connected by Vande Bharat Express, Amrit Bharat Express, and daily Superfast trains from Delhi, Lucknow, Varanasi, Gorakhpur, and Patna.

By Road

Ayodhya is connected via four-lane National Highway 27. It lies roughly 135 km east of Lucknow (2.5 hours by road) and 200 km northwest of Varanasi (4 hours). Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (UPSRTC) operates frequent AC Janrath and ordinary buses.

Local Transport

Ayodhya has extensively deployed eco-friendly electric auto-rickshaws (e-rickshaws) and battery-operated city buses along designated pilgrimage corridors (Ram Path, Bhakti Path, Janmabhoomi Path, and Dharam Path).

Where to Stay & Local Food Guidance

Where to Stay: For walking proximity to temples, consider accommodations situated along Ram Path, near Nayaghat, or around Tedhi Bazar. For quieter surroundings and modern hotels, properties situated near Ayodhya Airport bypass or toward Faizabad Cantt offer excellent vehicle access and dedicated parking.

Local Food Delicacies: Ayodhya's culinary tradition is purely vegetarian. Do not miss the famous Ayodhyawasi peda made from condensed milk, piping hot kachoris served with spicy aloo jhol for breakfast, fresh rabri with jalebi at Nayaghat, and pure ghee thalis available at historic annakshetras.

Essential Travel Tips for Ayodhya

  • Locker Facilities: Carry a small pouch for valuables; large backpacks and smartwatches are not allowed inside the main sanctum. Free digital locker counters are available near the pilgrim facilitation center.
  • Modest Clothing: Wear comfortable, modest clothing suited for walking and temple premises. Slip-on footwear is recommended as shoes must be removed before entering temples.
  • Wheelchair Assistance: The temple trust provides free wheelchairs and ramp access for elderly devotees and persons with disabilities.
  • Nearby Excursions: Combine your Ayodhya visit with Varanasi (200 km), Prayagraj (165 km), or Lucknow (135 km) for a comprehensive cultural circuit of Uttar Pradesh.
