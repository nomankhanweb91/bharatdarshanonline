Content Standards

BharatDarshan Editorial Policy & Principles

At BharatDarshan.online, our highest priority is publishing truthful, culturally sensitive, and practically actionable travel guidance for exploring India. This editorial charter details how our travel guides are researched, audited, and maintained.

1. People-First, Original Content

Every destination guide, state overview, and travel checklist is created with the genuine perspective of a traveler in mind. We answer foundational logistical questions: how to arrive safely, what cultural etiquettes apply inside sacred precincts, realistic time allocations, and how to avoid common pitfalls. We explicitly refuse to publish thin aggregator text or keyword-stuffed articles designed solely for search algorithms.

2. Rigorous Image Integrity & Landmark Accuracy

A core tenet of BharatDarshan is photographic truth. Each photograph displayed on a destination page must strictly depict that actual geographical destination or historical structure:

  • Ayodhya travel guides feature authentic imagery of the Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, or the sacred Saryu riverfront.
  • Agra guides feature verified photography of the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, or Fatehpur Sikri.
  • Jaipur guides feature the distinctive terracotta façades of Hawa Mahal or Amer Fort.
  • We prohibit the use of generic stock photos or unverified images that mislead readers about a monument's appearance.

3. Pricing Transparency & Realism

India's travel economy encompasses a wide spectrum of lodging, transport, and dining options. Where budget numbers are presented, they are categorized into clear tiers (Budget, Moderate, Premium) and clearly designated as realistic estimates rather than contractual guarantees. We advise travelers to account for seasonal variations such as peak winter festivals (Diwali, Holi, Dussehra, Kumbh Mela) and summer holiday spikes.

4. Proactive Verification of Transit & Official Regulations

Because railway schedules, flight frequencies, monument conservation closures by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI), and shrine darshan slots frequently change, our editorial workflow continually cross-references authoritative state tourism department notices and central ministry bulletins.

5. Corrections & Community Feedback

We welcome factual feedback from local residents, heritage scholars, and active travelers. When an error regarding an entry procedure, dress requirement, or route condition is identified, we promptly review and update the relevant guide.
