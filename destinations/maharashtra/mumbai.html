Mumbai Travel Guide: Gateway of India, Marine Drive & Heritage

Why Visit Mumbai?

Mumbai, previously known as Bombay, is a bustling archipelago of seven historic islands unified into a powerhouse metropolis along the Arabian Sea. As India's financial, commercial, and cinematic engine, Mumbai pulses with irrepressible energy.

The city boasts two distinct UNESCO World Heritage architecture ensembles: the Victorian Gothic and Art Deco Ensembles around Oval Maidan, and the rock-cut 5th-century Elephanta Caves in Mumbai Harbour. From evening breezes along Marine Drive's 3-kilometer sweep to the lively chaos of Colaba Causeway and Irani cafes serving bun maska and chai, Mumbai is an exhilarating sensory experience.

Top Places to Visit in Mumbai

1. Gateway of India & Taj Mahal Palace Hotel

Erected to commemorate the 1911 landing of King George V and Queen Mary, this 26-meter basalt arch fuses Roman triumphal design with 16th-century Gujarati decorative details. Beside it stands the iconic red-domed Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, opened in 1903.

2. Marine Drive (The Queen's Necklace)

A 3.6-kilometer C-shaped boulevard lining Back Bay. At night, sparkling streetlights form an arc resembling a string of pearls. It features the world's second-largest collection of Art Deco apartment buildings after Miami Beach.

3. Elephanta Caves Ferry from Gateway of India

Located on Gharapuri Island, an hour's scenic ferry ride across Mumbai Harbor. These rock-cut cave temples dating from the 5th to 7th centuries house monumental basalt sculptures of Lord Shiva, crowned by the colossal 6-meter-tall three-faced Trimurti (Sadashiva).

4. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) UNESCO World Heritage Site

Designed by F.W. Stevens and completed in 1887, this magnificent railway terminus is an exuberant masterpiece of Victorian High Gothic Revival architecture intertwined with traditional Indian palace stone carving.

5. Bandra & Bandra-Worli Sea Link

Traverse the 5.6-kilometer cable-stayed Sea Link bridge across the open sea into Bandra. Explore Portuguese heritage at Mount Mary Basilica, trendy seaside cafes on Carter Road, and Bollywood star residences along Bandstand promenade.

Suggested 3-Day Mumbai Itinerary

Day 1: South Mumbai Heritage Walk, Gateway & Marine Drive

Explore the Victorian architectural treasures around Fort, Kala Ghoda art precinct, and CSMT. Stroll through the Gateway of India plaza. In the late afternoon, enjoy sunset snacks at Chowpatty Beach and spend the twilight hours seated along the sea wall of Marine Drive.

Day 2: Elephanta Caves Excursion & Colaba Shopping

Take the 9:00 AM ferry from the Gateway jetty to Elephanta Island. Tour the ancient cave sculptures and return by early afternoon. Explore Colaba Causeway for antique books and handcrafted brassware, stopping at Leopold Cafe or Britannia & Co. for Parsi berry pulao.

Day 3: Bandra Heritage, Sea Link & Juhu Beach

Drive across the Bandra-Worli Sea Link to explore the colonial villages of Ranwar and Bandra West. Visit Mount Mary Church. In the late afternoon, head to Juhu Beach to experience Mumbai's famous beach street food culture before your departure.

How to Reach Mumbai

By Air

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) operates flights to all major Indian state capitals and direct non-stop international flights across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

By Train

Major terminals include CSMT, Mumbai Central, Bandra Terminus, and Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT), connecting Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, and express lines across India.

Local Mobility

Mumbai's extensive suburban railway network, modern underground Metro Line 3, black-and-yellow Premier Padmini cabs, and app-based rides provide comprehensive urban mobility.
