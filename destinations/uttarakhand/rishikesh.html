Rishikesh Travel Guide: Holy Ganga, Ashrams & Himalayan Adventure

Best Time to Visit September to April

Airport Dehradun Jolly Grant (DED)

Why Visit Rishikesh?

Rishikesh sits at the serene transition point where the pristine, emerald-green Ganga departs the Shivalik Himalayas to enter the northern plains. Revered since ancient Vedic times as a sacred abode of rishis and sages, it has rightfully earned its international title as the Yoga Capital of the World.

The town uniquely harmonizes peaceful spiritual contemplation with exhilarating mountain adventure: peaceful ashrams teaching Hatha yoga and Vedic philosophy, moving evening Ganga Aarti ceremonies at Parmarth Niketan and Triveni Ghat, grade III & IV white-water river rafting through Himalayan gorges, cliff jumping, bungee jumping in Mohan Chatti, and tranquil Ayurvedic wellness retreats.

Top Places to Visit in Rishikesh

1. Triveni Ghat & Evening Maha Aarti

The most sacred bathing ghat in Rishikesh, where the confluence of three mythological rivers (Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati) is venerated. At sunset, priests conduct an awe-inspiring Maha Aarti with chanting, conch shells, and hundreds of floating diya leaf boats drifting into the current.

2. Ram Jhula & Parmarth Niketan

An iconic 228-meter iron suspension footbridge connecting Shivananda Nagar to Swargashram. On the eastern bank lies Parmarth Niketan, one of Rishikesh's largest spiritual institutions, renowned for its serene courtyards, giant Lord Shiva statue resting in the river, and daily evening aarti on its private ghat.

3. Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia) Rajaji National Park

The legendary cliffside ashram where English rock band The Beatles stayed in 1968 under Maharishi Mahesh Yogi to study Transcendental Meditation, composing most of their famous White Album. Now managed by the forest department, it features graffiti art galleries, meditation stone igloos, and quiet jungle pathways.

4. White-Water River Rafting (Shivpuri to Rishikesh)

One of India's premier adventure experiences. The 16 km stretch from Shivpuri or the 24 km stretch from Marine Drive navigates thrilling Class III and IV rapids, including "Three Blind Mice," "Roller Coaster," "Golf Course," and "Club House."

5. Neer Garh Waterfall & Kunjapuri Temple

A scenic 5 km trek leads to the multi-tiered Neer Garh natural limestone waterfall. Further up at 1,665 meters lies Kunjapuri Devi Temple, which offers a breathtaking sunrise vista over snow-capped Himalayan peaks including Bandarpoonch, Chaukhamba, and Swargarohini.

Suggested 3-Day Rishikesh Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival, Ashram Check-in & Triveni Ghat Aarti

Arrive in Rishikesh. Check into your riverside hotel or ashram in Tapovan or Swargashram. Take an afternoon walking tour crossing Ram Jhula. In the evening, witness the soulful Ganga Maha Aarti at Triveni Ghat.

Day 2: Morning Yoga, Beatles Ashram & White-Water Rafting

Start with a 7:00 AM Hatha yoga session. Tour the historic meditation domes of the Beatles Ashram. In the afternoon, embark on an exhilarating 16-km river rafting expedition from Shivpuri down through the rapids to Lakshman Jhula.

Day 3: Kunjapuri Sunrise, Neer Waterfall & Cafes

Take an early 5:00 AM drive to Kunjapuri Temple to watch the morning sun crest the Himalayan snowline. Trek down past Neer Garh Waterfall. Spend the afternoon relaxing at the organic river-view cafes of Tapovan before your departure.

How to Reach Rishikesh

By Air

Dehradun's Jolly Grant Airport (DED) is located just 21 km from Rishikesh (35 minutes by taxi), offering daily flights connecting Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Lucknow, and Ahmedabad.

By Train

The state-of-the-art Yog Nagari Rishikesh (YNRK) terminal receives direct trains from Delhi, Jammu, and Kolkata. Additionally, Haridwar Junction (HW) (25 km away) provides frequent Vande Bharat and Shatabdi Express services.

By Road

The Delhi-Dehradun Expressway links New Delhi to Rishikesh (240 km) in approximately 4 to 5 hours. Direct AC Volvo buses operate continuously from Kashmiri Gate ISBT.
