Varanasi Travel Guide: Sacred Ghats, Kashi Vishwanath & Cultural Itinerary

Spectacular evening Ganga Aarti ceremony illuminated by multi-tiered brass lamps at Dashashwamedh Ghat, Varanasi.
State & Region Uttar Pradesh (Purvanchal)
Best Time to Visit October to March
Ideal Trip Duration 3 to 4 Days
Nearest Airport Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS)
Nearest Rail Terminals Varanasi Cantt (BSB) / Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya (DDU)
Primary Languages Hindi, Bhojpuri, English

Why Visit Varanasi (Kashi)?

Varanasi, also affectionately known as Kashi (City of Light) and Banaras, is universally recognized as one of humanity's oldest living cities. Set along the crescent-shaped western curve of the sacred River Ganga, the city represents the ultimate pilgrimage destination for Hindus, who seek spiritual emancipation (Moksha) in its sacred waters.

Varanasi is an evocative sensory tapestry: the resonant chime of temple bells at sunrise, silk weavers clacking wooden handlooms in narrow galis, classical Hindustani musicians rehearsing along riverside havelis, and the aromatic scent of Banarasi paan and saffron-infused rabri lassi.

Top Places to Visit in Varanasi

1. Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple & Corridor Open: 3:00 AM – 11:00 PM

Enshrining one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, the historic Kashi Vishwanath temple features towering golden spires. The modernized Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor seamlessly links the temple sanctum directly to the Ganga riverfront at Lalita Ghat, providing expansive marble plazas, escalators, and state-of-the-art pilgrim facilitation centers.

2. Dashashwamedh Ghat & Evening Ganga Aarti Aarti Daily at Sunset

The most prominent and vibrant ghat in Varanasi. Every evening at dusk, young priests dressed in saffron dhotis perform the choreographed Maha Aarti, lifting multi-tiered brass lamps weighing several kilograms while thousands of pilgrims watch from tiered stone steps and wooden boats on the river.

3. Sunrise Boat Ride (Assi to Manikarnika Ghat) Best: 5:30 AM – 7:30 AM

An early morning hand-rowed or electric boat ride provides the definitive vista of Varanasi. Watch the golden rays of the rising sun illuminate the grand palaces, Maratha stone ramparts, and bathing pilgrims greeting Surya with folded hands.

4. Sarnath Buddhist Pilgrimage Center 10 km from City Center

The serene site where Lord Buddha preached his first sermon after attaining enlightenment (Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta). Marvel at the 43-meter-tall Dhamek Stupa, inspect the ancient ruins of the Mulagandha Kuti Vihara, and visit the Sarnath Archaeological Museum, which houses the original 3rd-century BCE Ashoka Lion Capital (India's national emblem).

5. Assi Ghat & Subah-e-Banaras Daily: 5:00 AM

Located at the southernmost confluence of the Assi River and the Ganga, this ghat is renowned for the morning "Subah-e-Banaras" cultural program, comprising Vedic havan, morning aarti, classical shehnai concerts, and mass yoga sessions on the riverfront.

Suggested 3-Day Varanasi Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival, Ancient Alleyways & Dashashwamedh Aarti

Arrive in Varanasi and check into your hotel. In the afternoon, take a guided walk through the historic narrow galis of Old Kashi, sampling Banarasi kachori-sabzi and creamy malaiyo. Head to Dashashwamedh Ghat by 5:30 PM to secure prime seating for the world-renowned evening Ganga Aarti.

Day 2: Sunrise River Cruise, Kashi Vishwanath & Sarnath Excursion

Embark on a sunrise boat ride from Assi Ghat to Manikarnika Ghat. After breakfast, enter the Kashi Vishwanath Corridor for holy darshan and explore Annapurna Temple. In the afternoon, drive 10 km northeast to Sarnath to tour the Dhamek Stupa and the Archaeological Museum.

Day 3: BHU Vishwanath Temple, Ramnagar Fort & Silk Shopping

Visit the New Vishwanath Temple (Birla Temple) inside Banaras Hindu University (BHU). Cross the river to explore the 18th-century sandstone battlements of Ramnagar Fort. Spend the afternoon browsing authentic Banarasi silk sarees in Thatheri Bazar before departure.

How to Reach Varanasi

By Air

Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS) at Babatpur is 25 km northwest of the city center. It offers direct daily flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, and international flights to Kathmandu and Bangkok.

By Train

Varanasi is a paramount rail hub. Varanasi Junction (BSB), Banaras Station (BSBS), and Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Junction (DDU) (16 km away) host numerous Vande Bharat Express, Rajdhani, and express services nationwide.

By Road

Located on National Highway 19 (formerly Grand Trunk Road). Varanasi connects seamlessly with Prayagraj (125 km), Lucknow (300 km), and Ayodhya (200 km) via high-speed four-lane express corridors.

Local Mobility

Electric autos (e-rickshaws), cycle-rickshaws, and walking are best for navigating the dense old city, where regular cars are prohibited during peak hours.

Where to Stay & Food Specialties

Where to Stay: Travelers desiring direct sunrise vistas and immediate access to the Ganga Aarti should stay in restored heritage havelis near Assi Ghat or Dashashwamedh Ghat. Those seeking vehicle parking, modern elevators, and spacious grounds should choose the Cantonment area.

Must-Try Food Delicacies: Varanasi is famous for its distinct morning breakfast of piping hot Poori-Sabzi with Jalebi, rich Malaiyo (seasonal saffron milk foam topped with pistachios available in winter), refreshing Kashi Chaat Bhandar's Tamatar Chaat, and refreshing Banarasi sweet paan.
