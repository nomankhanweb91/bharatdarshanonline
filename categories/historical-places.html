Practical Advice for Visiting India's Historical Monuments

  • Online Ticketing via ASI: For all monuments protected by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) including Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Qutub Minar, and Fatehpur Sikri, purchase electronic tickets on the official ASI portal to bypass congested ticketing windows.
  • Weekly Closures: Remember standard monument closing days: the Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays; the Red Fort and many Delhi museums are closed on Mondays.
  • Government-Licensed Tour Guides: Look for guides wearing official photo badges issued by the Ministry of Tourism to avoid unauthorized touts offering misleading historical commentary.
